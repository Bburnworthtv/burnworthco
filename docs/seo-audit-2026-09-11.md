## What search checks establish

Public search checks for `site:burnworthco.com`, `"burnworthco.com"` and `"Burnworth Co" SEO` did not surface useful owned-page results in the available search tool. An unrelated network listing and similarly named entities appeared. This is a weak-discoverability signal, **not proof that Google has indexed zero pages** and not a live Google position measurement. No Google Maps grid, Search Console data, backlink index or direct ChatGPT/Copilot benchmark was available in this task.

Use Search Console URL inspection to determine actual index coverage. Do not describe this audit as a 100-query Google or AI ranking study. The web retrieval tool initially failed on the homepage, while direct HTTPS retrieval succeeded; this does not establish that Googlebot or OAI-SearchBot is blocked.

## Measurement specification

- Search: indexed coverage and non-branded clicks/impressions on priority service pages, with date range and source.
- Leads: genuine qualified inquiries, service requested, lead quality and confirmed attribution where available.
- Local: only for an eligible profile; record actual search coordinates, date and query when testing positions.
- AI mentions: answers naming Burnworth Co. / successfully completed answers.
- AI citations: answers citing burnworthco.com / successfully completed answers.
- Brand accuracy: correct descriptions / answers describing the brand.
- Keep exact prompts, platform/model where shown, time, location settings, search availability, cited URLs and results. Separate branded from unbranded questions.
- Failed or unavailable tests are missing observations, not negative results. Report sample sizes. A citation count is not a fixed rank.

## Validation and limitations

Dependency-free static checks validate twelve HTML documents including the 404, eleven sitemap URLs, unique titles/descriptions, self-canonicals, one H1 per page, local links/fragments/assets, manifest icons and JSON-LD. JavaScript syntax is checked separately.

Not measured: live improvements, indexing changes, Core Web Vitals, Lighthouse scores, real-user mobile behavior, conversion lifts, booking completion, authenticated platform settings or live server-side overrides. The two project pages intentionally provide context, not fabricated case-study outcomes. No awards or aggregate ratings were invented.

## Primary guidance reviewed

- [Google: optimizing for generative AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) — existing SEO principles, original value, crawlability and no special AI markup requirement.
- [Google: website controls and insights, updated August 31, 2026](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/new-controls-website-owners/) — current inclusion controls and reporting rollout.
- [Bing: AI Performance, February 10, 2026](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview) — citation reporting and its interpretation.
- [Google: local ranking factors](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091) — relevance, distance and prominence.
- [Google: HTTP status handling](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/troubleshooting/http-status-codes) — missing content must not masquerade as a successful page.
- [Cloudflare: serving Pages](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/configuration/serving-pages/) — clean HTML URLs and default SPA fallback without a root 404.
- [Pew: July 22, 2025 study](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) — historical observed behavior, not a forecast or a universal 2026 click-through rate.

There is no guaranteed rank or citation outcome. The prepared work improves specific controllable weaknesses and establishes a defensible basis for measuring what happens after publication.
