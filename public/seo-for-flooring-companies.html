Refinishing and installation are different searches.

Burnworth Co. does SEO for flooring contractors, installers and showrooms. Brandon Burnworth plans the work and builds it himself, from Oceanside, California.

A homeowner who wants an oak floor refinished and one shopping for new vinyl plank are making different decisions. Each needs its own page: what the job involves, which materials you work with, where you do it and how to ask for an estimate. A single “Services” page answers neither.

The same goes for patterns and materials. A herringbone installation, a tile job and a carpet replacement each answer a different question, and each is searched for by name.

What we did for Top Tier Custom Floors.

Top Tier Custom Floors is a flooring business based in Valley Center, serving San Diego County, including Rancho Santa Fe, Carmel Valley, Carlsbad and Oceanside. We are in Oceanside ourselves: see SEO for contractors in San Diego.

Before the work, owner Stone Peters says the website had never produced a job. Within two months of the SEO work he reported roughly three to four calls and estimate opportunities a week. Today the business reports landing 6 to 10 jobs a month, with quality leads coming in almost daily.

What flooring SEO includes.

  • Service pages for each job you sell, such as hardwood installation, refinishing, vinyl plank, tile and carpet.
  • Service-area pages built on jobs you have done in each town.
  • Google Business Profile: the right primary category, services, photos and a simple way to ask for reviews.
  • Project pages from your own before-and-after photos, with the material, pattern and location.
  • Tracking that separates real estimate requests from clicks.
  • AI answers (GEO and AEO): the questions flooring customers ask, answered plainly, so Google’s AI summaries and tools like ChatGPT can name you. Included in every package.

This is the flooring version of our SEO program for contractors. If the site itself is the problem, see website builds.

Showrooms and installers are searched differently.

Some flooring businesses sell materials, some install, and some do both. Google treats a flooring store and a flooring contractor as different kinds of business, and customers search for them differently.

Model Home, Inc. in San Marcos is a flooring contractor with a cabinet showroom. It reports consistently landing new remodeling leads in a highly competitive market. If you have a showroom and install crews, the site and the Business Profile need to say which one a customer is looking at.

A business based in one town and working in several needs the same care. Top Tier Custom Floors is based in Valley Center and works across North County. Its pages describe work in those towns without implying an office there.
