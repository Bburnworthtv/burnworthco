What it means

One job, several names.

GEO, AEO, AI SEO and LLM SEO all describe the same job: being the business an AI tool names when a customer asks. Here is what the three main names mean.

SEOSearch engine optimization
Showing up when someone searches Google or Maps for the work you do.
  • Google
  • Google Maps
  • Bing
AEOAnswer engine optimization
Being the answer when someone asks a full question instead of typing keywords.
  • Answer boxes on Google
  • People also ask
  • Voice and AI assistants
GEOGenerative engine optimization
Being named, and described correctly, when an AI tool writes the answer.
  • ChatGPT
  • Gemini
  • Perplexity
  • Google’s AI Overviews

What we do

What our GEO and AEO services include.

Call them GEO services, AEO services or AI SEO services: the work is the same. AI tools repeat what they can find, check and trust, so we make your business easy to find, check and trust.

  1. 01

    Baseline

    We ask the AI tools your customers use, such as ChatGPT and Google’s AI answers, the questions they ask, and record which businesses are named. That is the starting line.

  2. 02

    Answer-first pages

    Each service page answers the real question in its first sentences: what it costs, how long it takes, where you work.

  3. 03

    One set of facts

    Your name, services, service area, license and contact details match on your site, your Google Business Profile and your listings.

  4. 04

    Proof worth citing

    Your own projects, photos and reviews, written up with enough detail for people and AI tools to check.

  5. 05

    Open to the crawlers

    Pages that Google, Bing and the AI crawlers can reach and read, with structured data that matches what the page says.

  6. 06

    Tracking

    The same questions, asked again over time: who is named, which pages are cited, and the calls that followed.

Questions

Straight answers.

What owners ask about AI search before they start.

Brandon@burnworthco.com (208) 881-2382

What are AI search optimization services?

Work that helps a business get named, cited and described correctly in AI answers from ChatGPT, Google’s AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot. In practice that is clear answers on your service pages, the same business facts everywhere, proof worth citing, pages the crawlers can read, and a record of what the tools say.

Is AI SEO the same as GEO and AEO?

Mostly, yes. AI SEO, generative engine optimization (GEO), answer engine optimization (AEO) and LLM SEO are different names for getting found in AI answers. AI SEO can also mean using AI tools to do SEO work, which is a different thing. On this page it means being found.

How much do GEO and AEO services cost?

Nothing extra. GEO and AEO are included in every SEO package. Packages run $1,000 to $4,000 a month, depending on how many services you want to target, and the audit is free.

Can you get my business into ChatGPT’s answers?

No one can promise that, and we do not. AI answers change between runs and between people. We do the work that makes a business easy to find, check and cite, record what the tools say, and report the calls and estimate requests that follow.

Do I still need regular SEO?

Yes. Google’s AI answers draw on its search index, and OpenAI says sites that block its search crawler are not shown in ChatGPT search answers. A page has to be found before it can be quoted, so SEO comes first and the same work feeds both.

How do you measure AI visibility?

We agree on a fixed set of customer questions, ask them on each platform, and record the date, the businesses named and the pages cited. We ask the same questions again over time and report the trend beside your calls and estimate requests.

How long does it take?

It depends on your market and where your site starts. The audit records what the tools say today, so there is a starting point to measure from. We do not promise a date.

Are you a GEO, AEO or AI SEO agency?

We are a small studio focused on consistent client results. Brandon Burnworth plans the work and does it himself, and Zenande Burnworth runs timelines. You get the GEO, AEO and SEO work an agency sells, without account managers or handoffs.

Is this only for contractors?

No. Most of our clients are contractors and other local service businesses, and the work is the same for any business that customers look up before they call.
