AI visibility should
end in a phone call.

Being mentioned by an AI system is interesting. Being found by the right buyer is useful. The job is to connect both to demand a business can actually feel.

Brandon Burnworth6 min readClient outcomes reported September 2026

In this field note

The outcome What changed Where AI fits What to measure

01 · The outcome

Visibility only matters when the business notices.

Top Tier Custom Floors came to Burnworth Co. as an established San Diego County flooring business built primarily through referrals. Owner Stone Peters told us the website had never produced a job for the company.

After the SEO work, Stone now reports receiving roughly three to four calls and estimate opportunities per week through the website. Demand has increased enough that he says he sometimes has to turn work down.

A website that had never produced a job is now creating several calls and estimate opportunities in a typical week.

That is the result I care about. Rankings, impressions, citations and traffic are diagnostic signals. They are not the business outcome.

This is a client-reported before-and-after result for one business. It is not a guarantee that another company will see the same volume.

02 · What changed

The work was not one trick.

The strategy was conventional in the best sense of the word: make the business easier for search systems and customers to understand, then give each important service enough evidence to stand on its own.

  • Clarify the full business identity as Top Tier Custom Floors, not a vague shortened name.
  • Separate services so installation, refinishing, herringbone, concrete and epoxy answer different customer needs.
  • Connect real project work to the locations the company genuinely serves.
  • Improve technical SEO, structured information, internal linking and indexability.
  • Use first-hand project photographs and details instead of generic service copy.
  • Measure qualified calls and estimate requests, not traffic in isolation.

The same principle has worked in a very different market. A Los Angeles production client now reports first-place Google organic rankings across six camera-equipment rental searches, including searches for Towercam and robotic dolly rental in Los Angeles.

03 · Where AI fits

AI visibility is built on the same clarity.

AI search creates a new surface for discovery, but it does not remove the need for strong search fundamentals. A system still needs to identify the business, understand what it does, find useful supporting information and decide whether that information is worth surfacing.

Google's current guidance for generative search continues to emphasize the same fundamentals used in ordinary Search rather than a special set of “AI SEO” requirements. Bing now exposes AI citation reporting, including citation share, topics and intents, inside Webmaster Tools.

That changes what we can observe. It does not change what a business ultimately needs.

Visibility metricRankings · citations · impressions · mentions
Business metricCalls · estimates · qualified inquiries · revenue

An AI citation with no commercial relevance is not automatically valuable. A lower-volume query that repeatedly brings the right customer can be much more important.

For that reason, Burnworth Co. treats AI visibility as part of a larger search system rather than a separate product that lives on a dashboard.

04 · What to measure

Follow the chain all the way to the inquiry.

For a local service business, I want to know more than whether a page appeared. I want to know what happened after it appeared.

  1. Discovery: which non-branded searches and AI questions surfaced the company?
  2. Evidence: which service pages, project pages and outside sources supported that visibility?
  3. Action: did the visitor call, request an estimate or identify a specific service?
  4. Quality: was the inquiry in the right geography, for the right work and at a viable project value?
  5. Business effect: did enough qualified demand arrive to change the company's workload?
The strongest visibility report is the one the owner can recognize without opening the report.

That is why Top Tier's result matters more to me than a screenshot of a keyword position. The owner can feel the difference in the phone calls and estimates arriving each week.

AI visibility should be measured the same way. Track the citations. Track the rankings. Then keep going until you know whether the visibility created a business opportunity.
