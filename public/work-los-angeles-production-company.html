Equipment buyers search precisely.

This Los Angeles-based production company is a Burnworth Co. client working with specialist camera and motion systems. The website project centers on making the equipment easier for production teams to understand and inquire about.

Systems discussed in the project include MAT Towercam, AGITO, Ross Furio and ARRI SRH360. A production buyer may know the system name already, or may be comparing ways to achieve a particular camera move.

Technical detail helps the inquiry.

A useful equipment page should explain the system, its intended application, configuration considerations and the information needed to discuss a rental. Photographs should identify the actual equipment rather than illustrate a generic production scene.

Availability, crew needs and the final configuration depend on the production. Those details belong in a conversation with the rental team; a website should make that conversation easier to start.

Connect the system to the production.

The website and search work focuses on clear equipment names, navigable system information and relevant production imagery. Equipment-specific searches, such as Towercam rental or AGITO rental in Los Angeles, reflect a different intent from broad searches for video production services.

Brandon’s broadcast engineering and robotic camera background informs the questions behind the site: what the production needs to achieve, which system is being considered and what practical details the rental team needs.
