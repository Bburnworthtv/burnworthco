What happens after I answer the questions?

Brandon reviews your answers and your website, then emails a free audit: where your business shows up today, what is holding it back and what to fix first. You decide whether to talk after that.

How much does it cost?

The audit is free. Website builds run $2,000 to $5,000, depending on the number of pages and services. SEO programs run $1,000 to $4,000 a month, depending on how many services you want to target.

What are GEO and AEO, and do they cost extra?

GEO (generative engine optimization) is getting your business named when AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini write an answer. AEO (answer engine optimization) is being the answer when someone asks a full question, on Google or out loud. Both are included in every SEO package. Neither is sold as an add-on.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. SEO programs are planned for six months, the minimum is two, and after that you can cancel anytime. After six months it goes month to month.

How long until it works?

Your market, your competitors and where your site starts decide that. Top Tier Custom Floors started getting calls through its website within two months. We will tell you what we see in the audit.

Who will I work with?

Brandon Burnworth plans the work and builds it. Zenande Burnworth runs timelines and keeps you updated. There are no account managers.

Who owns the website and accounts?

You do. The website, domain, Google Business Profile, analytics and content are yours, and we transfer everything if you leave.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No, and nobody honestly can. Positions depend on competitors, how far you are from the searcher, and Google. We report calls and estimate requests, not promises.
