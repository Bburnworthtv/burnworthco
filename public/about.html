The story

Before search systems,
I built broadcast systems.

My career started in live television, where technical problems are public, deadlines are immovable, and the system has to work when millions of people are watching.

Since 2014, I’ve worked across broadcast engineering, camera systems, robotics, and IP production on some of the largest entertainment and live-event productions in the industry — including the Super Bowl Halftime Show, Dancing with the Stars, the Oscars, the Emmys, the Grammys, American Idol, America’s Got Talent, and other major productions.

That work taught me to think in systems: understand the signal path, isolate what is failing, build the right solution, and verify the result. Burnworth Co. applies the same approach to a different kind of technical system — the one connecting a business to its customers through the web, Google, and AI.

What carries over
