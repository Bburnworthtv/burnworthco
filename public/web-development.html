03 · Search

SEO is part of the build.

A site should not be built first and fixed for search later. We plan the page names, headings, links, speed, and structured data as the site is built.

One person can build the site and make sure search engines understand it.

See how this connects to local SEO and a search visibility audit. This gives your SEO and AI visibility work a stronger base from day one. It also makes later updates faster and easier.

04 · Process

From first plan to live site.

For a redesign, we first inventory the existing URLs and useful content. Pages that move need redirects; pages that already serve customers well should retain their purpose. Tracking and contact actions are checked as part of the launch.

  4. Launch. We test the site, connect the domain, and check the live pages.
