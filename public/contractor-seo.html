SEO for owner-operated contractors.

Burnworth Co. does SEO for contractors: flooring, remodeling and other trades where the owner still answers the phone. We are a two-person studio in Oceanside, California, and we work with contractors across the United States. Brandon Burnworth plans the work and builds it himself.

A homeowner searching for hardwood installation needs to see the service, the area you cover, examples of your work and a clear way to request an estimate. A general page about your company rarely answers all four. Contractor SEO is the work of building the pages and profiles that do.

What the work includes.

  • Search and competitor review: compare your priority services and real service areas with the businesses customers find first.
  • Service pages: one page per job you want more of, covering who it suits, the process, materials, limits and the next step.
  • Google Business Profile: the right categories, services, photos and a simple way to ask customers for reviews.
  • Business information: the same name, phone number and website on every profile and directory.
  • Project proof: your own photographs and job details, used to show what you do and where.
  • Technical fixes: crawl errors, page titles, internal links, canonical URLs, speed and mobile problems.
  • Trade directories and links: relevant trade bodies, supplier listings and genuine editorial opportunities.

Service areas need evidence.

A service-area page should help someone decide whether to hire you in that town. Useful details include real projects, travel or scheduling limits and the services you offer there. Changing a city name in otherwise identical text adds little.

For example, Top Tier Custom Floors is based in Valley Center and works in Rancho Santa Fe, Carmel Valley, Carlsbad and Oceanside. The business location and the service area are different things. Neither a service-area page nor a schema field creates a physical office.

Google describes local rankings in terms of relevance, distance and prominence. Website work can improve relevance and support reputation, but it cannot change how far a business is from a searcher. Read Google’s local ranking guidance.

What we measure.

We separate people searching your name from people finding you for the first time. Reports connect service-page visits with real inquiries, the job requested and whether it fits your business.

A tap on the call button shows interest. It does not prove a call happened. A submitted form is not automatically a good lead. We agree on those distinctions before reporting growth.

The numbers that matter are non-branded search clicks, impressions on your priority pages, Business Profile actions and qualified inquiries by service. Read how we record evidence.

Google and AI answers.

Customers now ask ChatGPT and Google’s AI summaries who to call. Those tools read the same things Google does: clear service pages, consistent business facts, reviews and third-party listings. The contractor SEO work covers both.

We record what the tools say instead of claiming a rank. On November 23, 2026, we asked ChatGPT “Who are the best flooring contractors near Valley Center, CA?” and it listed Top Tier Custom Floors first. Asked where to rent robotic camera systems for a live production in Los Angeles, it listed our production client first. Each was a single run with web search on. Answers change between runs, and not every question names a client.
