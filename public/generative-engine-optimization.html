What are GEO
and AEO?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) and answer engine optimization (AEO) are the work of showing up when people ask a question instead of typing a search. You may also hear them called AI SEO or AI search optimization. Both build on SEO, and both are included in every SEO package we offer.

01 · Definitions

Three names for getting found.

For years, getting found meant ranking on Google. Now people also ask full questions, out loud or in a chat window, and get one answer back instead of a list of links. Two newer names describe the work of being in that answer.

02 · AEO

What is answer engine optimization (AEO)?

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the work of making your page the direct answer when someone asks a question. An answer engine gives one answer instead of a page of links. Google has done this for years with the answer box at the top of a results page, the “People also ask” list, and voice assistants that read a single result aloud. AI assistants now do the same in a chat window.

For a service business, that means answering the questions customers really ask, in plain words, where a machine can read them:

  • Start from real questions. The ones you hear on the phone and see in reviews and in Search Console: what it costs, how long it takes, whether you work in their town.
  • Answer in the first sentence or two. Then give the detail. A short, direct answer under a clear question is easy to quote.
  • Put each answer on the page that sells that service. A refinishing question belongs on the refinishing page, not in one long list of questions.
  • Keep the facts in text. Hours, service area, license number and price ranges should be words on the page, not part of an image.
  • Say the same thing everywhere. The answer on your site should match your Google Business Profile and your listings.

03 · GEO

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the work of getting your business named, cited and described correctly in answers written by AI tools. ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews are generative engines: they read many sources, then describe and compare businesses in their own words. An AI answer may use search results, product feeds, trusted data, and public web pages.

GEO helps AI tools find, understand and choose your brand. It makes the facts clear so the tools can check your claims and cite your pages. AEO is about being quoted. GEO is about being named, and described correctly.

SEO helps your pages get found. AEO helps them get quoted. GEO helps your brand get named and described correctly.

Good SEO still matters. Pages should be fast, useful, easy to crawl, and easy to reach through links. GEO also checks how other sources describe a brand and whether its information is useful and original enough to cite.

That is why we do not sell them separately. A page that answers a customer’s question clearly helps in the search results, in the answer box and in an AI answer at the same time.

05 · Other names

AI SEO, LLM SEO and the other names.

The same work is sold under many names. If you have seen one of these, this is what it means.

AI SEO
Usually the same as GEO: getting found in AI answers. It can also mean using AI tools to do SEO work, which is a different thing.
AI search optimization
The plain-English name for GEO and AEO together. It is the name we use for the service.
LLM SEO, LLMO
LLM stands for large language model, the technology behind tools like ChatGPT and Gemini. The work is the same as GEO.
GAIO
Generative AI optimization. Another name for GEO.
ChatGPT SEO
GEO aimed at one tool. The work is the same for the others.
AI Overview optimization
GEO and AEO aimed at the AI answers at the top of a Google results page.
Geo-targeting
Not the same thing. In local SEO, “geo” means showing up in a particular town or area. That is service-area work.

06 · How to

How to show up in ChatGPT and Google’s AI answers.

There is no trick, and no one can guarantee a place in an answer. These are the things that make a business easy for an AI tool to find, check and cite in 2026.

  1. Let the crawlers in. OpenAI says sites that block its search crawler, OAI-SearchBot, are not shown in ChatGPT search answers. Google’s AI answers use pages in Google’s index. Check that your robots.txt file and your host are not blocking them.
  2. Put key facts in clear text. Do not hide your main offer in images, forms, scripts, or pages that need a login.
  3. Keep brand facts the same everywhere. Use the same company name, people, services, location, and contact details on your site and trusted profiles.
  4. Publish something useful and new. Share real experience, your own data, clear methods, and fair comparisons. These give AI tools a reason to cite you.
  5. Help with the full buying choice. Explain choices, limits, steps, proof, and what to do next. Do not make thin pages for each keyword.
  6. Support claims. Link to original sources. Explain how you got the facts. Name the writer and update old details.
  7. Earn real mentions. AI tools draw on more than your own site. Listings with trade bodies and suppliers, reviews and genuine press give them more than one place to confirm who you are and what you do. Bought or fake mentions do not help.
  8. Keep search tools up to date. Keep your sitemap and main page links correct. Use Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools to inspect discovery. IndexNow can notify participating engines of changed URLs; it does not submit URLs to Google or guarantee indexing.

What not to rely on

Google says there is no special AI schema required and that `llms.txt`, forced content chunking, pages for every query variation, and inauthentic mentions do not provide a Google generative-search advantage. Structured data is still useful when it accurately describes visible information and supports established search features.

07 · Measurement

Track visibility and sales.

A useful plan tracks both SEO results and AI visibility:

  • Indexed coverage and non-branded rankings
  • Brand inclusion for controlled prompt sets
  • Citation share and cited URLs
  • Accuracy and sentiment of brand descriptions
  • AI referral quality and assisted conversions
  • Branded-search growth and qualified pipeline

Our audit method records the exact questions, platforms, dates, mentions and cited URLs so repeated samples can be compared. Results should be viewed as trends. AI answers can change for each person and search. No one can promise a fixed answer or a permanent citation.

Review the generative AI inclusion setting and available reports in Search Console. Google’s August 31, 2026 update describes the worldwide rollout of these controls and insights. Bing’s AI Performance reporting provides a separate view of citations on supported experiences.

09 · Questions

Questions people ask about GEO and AEO.

What does GEO stand for?

GEO stands for generative engine optimization: the work of getting a business named, cited and described correctly in answers written by AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews. In local SEO, “geo” can also mean geo-targeting, which is a different thing.

What does AEO stand for?

AEO stands for answer engine optimization: the work of making your page the direct answer when someone asks a question, whether that answer appears in a box on Google, is read out by a voice assistant or is written by an AI assistant.

What is the difference between GEO and SEO?

SEO gets your pages into the list of results on Google and Maps. GEO gets your business named inside the answer an AI tool writes. GEO builds on SEO: a page has to be found before it can be cited.

What is the difference between AEO and SEO?

SEO earns your page a place in the list of results. AEO earns the direct answer: the box above the list, or the single answer a voice or AI assistant gives. AEO builds on SEO, because an answer is taken from a page that was found first.

What is the difference between AEO and GEO?

AEO is about your words being used as the direct answer. GEO is about your business being named and described correctly when an AI tool writes its own answer. Many people use the two names for the same thing, and the work overlaps.

Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. Google’s AI answers draw on its search index, so a page still has to be crawled, indexed and worth ranking. GEO and AEO add to SEO. They do not replace it.

Is AI SEO the same as GEO?

Usually. AI SEO, AI search optimization, LLM SEO and GEO are names for getting found in AI answers. AI SEO can also mean using AI tools to do SEO work, which is something else.

How do I get my business to show up in ChatGPT?

Make sure ChatGPT’s search crawler can read your site, answer your customers’ questions plainly on your service pages, keep your business facts the same everywhere, and earn real mentions on other sites. No one can guarantee a place in the answer.

Do GEO and AEO cost extra?

Not with us. Both are included in every SEO package, and the audit is free.
